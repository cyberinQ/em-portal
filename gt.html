Project GHOST-TRACK (GT)

Multi-Spectral Passive Detection & Anomaly Correlation Engine

CyberinQ specializes in combining multiple disparate data streams simultaneously to expose tiny, hidden anomalies and correlations that are invisible to any single sensor alone. The primary application of this methodology is Ghost Track, a highly sensitive, un-jammable detection and tracking system. GT is designed to reliably locate and monitor fast-moving, stealth-elusive targets - such as hypersonic glide vehicles, low-RCS airframes, and advanced maritime threats - across the space, atmospheric, and oceanic domains.

Traditional tracking systems rely on active radar architectures that emit powerful electromagnetic signals and measure the backscatter return. Ghost Track abandons this approach. Instead, we treat the massive volume of ambient civilian radio waves, satellite signals, and background noise that already fills the sky as our passive tracking network, exploiting the faint atmospheric wakes and minuscule electromagnetic perturbations that fast-moving masses inevitably leave behind. By constantly monitoring several independent, multi-spectral signal sources simultaneously, our trained models and agents instantly flag when a real physical mass forces these separate frequencies to shift in lockstep. This allows us to map a target’s exact trajectory with high precision without ever revealing our own location.

Deployment & Consumption Model

Ghost Track is purpose-built for Size, Weight, Power, and Cost (SWaP-C) constrained, contested, and austere environments. The system operates under a highly flexible, dual-mode framework designed for seamless operational integration:

  • Forensic Analysis Mode: GT can ingest historical, archived background datasets to perform post-event reconstruction. This mode allows operators to uncover previously undetected target paths, expose adversary electronic warfare tactics, and isolate exact launch or origin coordinates through deep retrospective processing.
  • Live Operational Mode: For real-time threat monitoring, GT runs at the tactical edge. It processes live, un-degraded ambient data streams completely out-of-band, providing an untraceable, continuous tracking canvas.

Because the system shifts the heavy lifting from specialized hardware to software-defined consistency validation, the live deployment footprint requires no proprietary tracking hardware, no high-power emitters, and no expensive hardware-based atomic clock synchronization. Ghost Track deploys directly onto standard Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) edge-computing architecture and utilizes freely available, open-access geodetic and environmental data streams - delivering a scalable strategic defense capability with minimal infrastructure overhead and deployment-operating-maintenance costs.
